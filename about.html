Training with purpose.

Task Karate is a local martial arts school built around steady progress, practical skill, and a community that helps people keep showing up.

Meet your instructors

A focused, welcoming school.

Randy Thomson leads Task Karate with a focus on fundamentals, confidence-building, and helping students understand why the work matters.

The school offers American Karate, IS3 Eskrima, weapons, sparring, and related training for kids, teens, and adults in the La Crosse area.

  • Learn in a structured, supportive environment
  • Build confidence through practice and repetition
  • Train with respect for partners and instructors
