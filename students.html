The dojo handbook

Start with good habits.

What should I bring?

Wear comfortable clothing and bring water. Ask the school what uniform or equipment is appropriate for your program.

How does belt progress work?

Progress is based on training, skill, effort, and instructor guidance. Use the belt sheets as a study resource, then confirm current testing expectations with your instructor.

Where can I find current class times?

The schedule page is the best public starting point. Special sessions and testing are announced by the school.
